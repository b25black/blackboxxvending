FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about our AI-powered smart vending machines.

What is an AI Vending Machine?

An AI vending machine (or smart cooler) uses computer vision, cameras, and deep learning sensors to identify products automatically. Customers unlock the door via a payment method, grab any item, and close the door. The machine automatically detects what was taken and charges their account without needing product scanning or push-buttons.

How accurate is the AI product recognition?

Most modern AI vending systems achieve a 98% to 99%+ accuracy rate using trained computer vision models. If an item is incorrectly billed, cloud management software allows operators to review session footage and process refunds instantly.

What happens if a customer puts an item back?

The AI tracks the customer's session in real time. If an item is picked up and then returned to the shelf before the door shuts, it is automatically removed from the running cart. The customer is charged only for what they leave with.

What happens if the machine loses internet connection?

The machine is designed to handle temporary internet interruptions. Product activity and transaction details can be stored locally and synced once the connection is restored. If connectivity affects payment authorization or monitoring, the operator is alerted so service can be restored quickly.

Will the vending machine be insured?

Yes. The vending machine is fully insured, providing coverage for the equipment while it is placed on-site.

Who restocks the machine and how often?

The machine is re-stocked and serviced by the operator. Restocking frequency depends on demand and product movement, but inventory is monitored remotely so popular items can be replenished before they run out.
